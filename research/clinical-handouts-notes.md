## Foods with cross-source disagreement

Recorded per food in the JSON `disagreements` array (100 foods). The most consequential:

- **avocado**: Stanford/GastroClinic avoid; Kaiser 1/8 (but also in its avoid list); MNGI 1/8; IBS Diets 60 g; Kate 3 slices; CUH/Glos <1/4; FE and Kate grocery list unlimited.
- **sweet potato**: 1/3 cup (CDHF) vs 1/2 cup/75 g (IBS Diets, Kaiser, MNGI, CUH, ALBY) vs "very small" (Glos); ALBY moderate at 100 g.
- **broccoli**: Stanford limit; IBS Diets/CDHF heads 3/4 cup; Kaiser/MNGI 1/2 cup; Glos <3 tbsp; Kate/ACG/FE/CUH unlimited.
- **almond**: 10 nuts (IBS Diets, CUH, Glos, FE3) vs handful (Kate) vs unlimited (Stanford). Almond flour: IBS Diets high, FE low.
- **oat milk**: 30-50 ml only (IBS Diets, CUH, Glos) vs avoid (Kaiser) vs low (FE).
- **quinoa**: Stanford caps at 1/2 cup if symptomatic; others 3/4-1 cup or unlimited.
- **coconut** (flesh, milk, water, cream): Stanford limits all; ACG carton milk high; CDHF coconut water high; Monash-based lists give 100-125 ml / 1/4-2/3 cup serves.
- **tomato products**: fresh universally low; paste 2 tbsp (IBS Diets) vs avoid (Kaiser, MNGI); sun-dried 4 pieces (IBS Diets, CUH) vs avoid (Kate, Kaiser, MNGI); tomato juice 1/2 cup vs high (CDHF); ketchup 1 tbsp vs avoid (Kaiser, GastroClinic).
- **sourdough (wheat)**: CDHF 2 slices, MNGI 1 slice, Kate/FE/ACG low; CUH/Glos/UHS allow only 100% spelt sourdough.
- **wheat bread / wheat pasta**: IBS Diets and FE give a small low serve (1 slice, 1/2 cup cooked); every hospital handout says avoid.
- **spelt**: Stanford/CUH/Glos treat as wheat; FE/Kaiser allow spelt flour.
- **cabbage**: Stanford and Kaiser avoid; all Monash-based lists 1/2-3/4 cup or unlimited.
- **bell pepper**: CDHF red/yellow/orange high (excess fructose), ALBY red 1/3 cup; Kaiser/Stanford/Kate all colors unlimited.
- **grape**: CDHF main page high (excess fructose) vs CDHF "What can I eat" low; IBS Diets 10-15 grapes; others unlimited.
- **strawberry**: 5 berries (ALBY, IBS Diets, Monash retest) vs unlimited (Stanford, Kate, Kaiser, CDHF, JH).
- **blueberry**: 1 cup (IBS Diets, CDHF) vs 1/2 cup (Kaiser) vs 1/4 cup (MNGI).
- **ripe banana**: group given as fructans (CDHF, Monash-based), fructose (Cleveland Clinic), polyols (FE3).
- **grapefruit**: Stanford low; Kate/Kaiser/ACG/CDHF high; IBS Diets/CUH/Glos half a fruit.
- **pomegranate, lychee, honeydew, papaya, guava, cranberry, date**: small-serve low in some Monash lists vs avoid in others; Stanford limits papaya and guava; CDHF lists honeydew high and lychee low.
- **canned legumes** (chickpea 1/4 cup, lentil 1/4-1/2 cup, black bean 1/4 cup, kidney bean 100 g CUH): allowed by Monash-based lists and CUH; Stanford, Kaiser, JH, UHS, Glos, Mayo say avoid all beans/lentils.
- **edamame**: IBS Diets/MNGI high; Kate, CUH, CDHF, ACG, FE low at ~1/2 cup.
- **tofu**: CDHF "What can I eat" lists tofu high; all others firm tofu low, silken high.
- **soy milk**: Kaiser/MNGI/ACG/CDHF high; IBS Diets/Kate/FE low if made from soy protein; CUH/Glos 50-60 ml.
- **miso, TVP**: Kaiser avoids; CUH/Glos/IBS Diets/FE allow.
- **dairy edge cases**: sour cream, half and half, cream cheese, cottage cheese, ricotta, kefir, Greek yogurt, American cheese, whipped cream, brie: 2 tbsp/1 slice allowed by IBS Diets, Kaiser, CUH, Glos vs avoid by Stanford, Kate, MNGI, CDHF, FE. Kefir: IBS Diets/Kaiser high, MNGI allowed, Kate grocery lists plain Lifeway kefir.
- **sweeteners**: erythritol low (IBS Diets) vs avoid (Kaiser, MNGI, CUH); corn syrup allowed (Kaiser, MNGI) vs high (CDHF); molasses/treacle avoid (Kaiser, MNGI, CUH) vs treacle suitable (Glos) vs small amounts OK (Kate); cocoa powder 1 tbsp vs avoid (Kaiser); carob high (IBS Diets, MNGI) vs 1 tsp (CUH).
- **beverages**: kombucha high (IBS Diets, Kate, Kaiser) vs 180 ml (CUH) vs low (FE 2017); instant coffee avoid (Kaiser) vs low (IBS Diets, FE); orange juice 100 ml vs high (CDHF) vs low (FE); diet soda low (ACG, IBS Diets) vs high (CDHF); malted chocolate drink high (IBS Diets) vs low (FE).
- **alcohol**: sweet wine low (FE) vs dessert wine avoid (Kaiser, CUH, Glos).
- **condiments**: pesto <1 tbsp (IBS Diets) vs high (IBS Diets chart, UHS); wasabi high (IBS Diets) vs low (CUH, FE, Kate); stock cubes low (FE) vs high (everyone else); pickle limit (Stanford) vs dill pickle low (MNGI); jam limit (Stanford) vs 1.5-2 tbsp (IBS Diets, Kaiser, MNGI).
- **vegetable oddities**: mushroom (FE lists fresh button low); beet (FE lists beets low; others fresh high, canned/pickled low); pumpkin (CDHF high vs NHS suitable); sauerkraut (IBS Diets/CDHF small serve vs MNGI/CUH avoid); asparagus and sugar snap pea (Glos prints in both columns); cassava/taro (IBS Diets high vs CUH/FE low); celery (Stanford/Kate/FE unlimited vs <5 cm); green pea (Kaiser 1/2 cup vs avoid); snow pea (5 pods vs avoid); brussels sprout (2-3 sprouts vs avoid); savoy cabbage (FE low vs high); zucchini/summer squash (65 g cap vs unlimited; Stanford limits summer squash); okra/fennel/butternut (Stanford limit vs 1/4-1/2 cup).
- **Cheerios**: Stanford/Kate grocery/MNGI allow; Gloucestershire NHS avoids (UK formulation contains wheat).
- **chili powder/chipotle**: MNGI avoids dried chipotle; IBS Diets lists chipotle powder low.
- **hazelnut**: IBS Diets page says 24, its own chart and CUH/Glos say 10.

## Method notes

- Ratings in the JSON: `high` = avoid at a normal serving; `moderate` = low only at the restricted `lowServing`; `low` = low at a typical serving; `null` = not a source of that group or not stated.
- Where a handout only said "high FODMAP", the group was inferred from Monash-published composition and flagged "group inferred" or "inferred" in notes (e.g. rum, kombucha, protein bar, sushi, cocktail, hard cider).
- Serving sizes are quoted per source with attribution because they conflict; treat them as per-meal (ALBY: same food again after 3-4 hours).
- Fetch failures: hopkinsmedicine.org (Cloudflare), mayoclinic.org domains (Akamai 403, not archived), michiganmedicine.org health-lab (JS challenge), med.umich.edu patient library (login). Wayback Machine copies used where they existed.
